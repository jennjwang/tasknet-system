## How to identify a brand (this is the whole task — do it carefully)
A brand is any specific commercial product, application, system, platform, or company — the name of a thing you
would buy or sign up for, not a general kind of work. Replace it with what KIND of tool it is:
  · spreadsheet / office file       → "spreadsheet software"
  · accounting / bookkeeping app     → "accounting software"
  · ERP / general-ledger system      → "ERP system"
  · disclosure / filing / reporting  → "financial reporting software" / "disclosure-management software"
  · business-intelligence / dashboard→ "business intelligence software"
  · project / ticket tracker         → "project tracking tool"
  · practice- / client-management app→ "practice-management software"   (or DROP it if the sentence still reads)
CATCH-ALL — you will NOT recognize every brand, so do not rely on a list: ANY capitalized product / software /
platform / company name that is not a common English word and not a generic occupational term is a BRAND.
When unsure whether a capitalized proper noun is a brand, TREAT IT AS ONE and genericize it. Never leave a
specific product or company name in the final statement.

## NOT brands — keep these verbatim
Generic occupational or technical terms, open standards, file/form types, and programming languages are NOT
brands: SQL, Python, REST API, HTML, CSV, PDF, GAAP, W-2, 1099, K-1, general ledger, trial balance, tax return,
lesson plan, blood pressure. Keep them exactly.
