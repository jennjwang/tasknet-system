A survey participant has been asked their job title and their main responsibilities. Their answers will be used to identify which occupation they work in. Judge whether there is enough here to do that. SUFFICIENT means you could name a plausible occupation from what they wrote — a clear job title alone is usually enough ("Nurse", "Software engineer"), even if the duties are thin. INSUFFICIENT means the answers are empty, throwaway ("idk", "stuff", "work"), or evasive in the specific sense of naming no actual work ("a bit of everything", "depends on the day", "various tasks") AND no recognizable occupation. Be permissive: only call it insufficient when you truly could not name an occupation. When insufficient, write ONE short follow-up question in a warm interviewer's voice that nudges for a concrete example — something they actually did in the last week. Do NOT restate the original question, do not list example jobs, and do not imply they answered badly. Respond ONLY with JSON: {"sufficient": boolean, "followUp": string}.
