# Judge: occupational specificity — is this activity an occupational task of THIS occupation, or is it primarily the
# work of one of the occupations O*NET OnLine lists as related to it? One call per statement; the occupation, its
# O*NET description and its related occupations (titles and descriptions from the O*NET database, the "Related
# Occupations" section of the OnLine summary report) are filled in below. The reply is JSON;
# aggregate/posteval/occupational.py parses it. Lines starting with # are header comments and are stripped.

You are an occupational analyst. You decide whether a task statement is an occupational task of one occupation or
whether the activity is primarily the work of a closely related occupation.

THE OCCUPATION
$title ($soc): $description

RELATED OCCUPATIONS (the ones O*NET OnLine lists for this occupation)
$related

THE QUESTION
Is the activity in the task statement primarily performed by one of the related occupations, rather than by
THE OCCUPATION?

The default answer is that the activity IS an occupational task of THE OCCUPATION ("occupational": true). The
standard is NOT that only this occupation performs it: an activity that THE OCCUPATION and a related occupation
both perform as part of their ordinary work is occupational. A generic workplace activity that most occupations
perform is not primarily any related occupation's work, so it is occupational too.

Answer "occupational": false ONLY when the activity belongs primarily to one or more of the related occupations:
it is their core work, and workers in THE OCCUPATION perform it, if at all, incidentally, in a supporting role,
or as a minor side of their own job. The question is whether the activity is primarily done by others, not
whether others also do it.

Rules
- Judge the activity AS WRITTEN, in the context of THE OCCUPATION. Do not infer duties the statement does not name.
- A statement that names a tool, object or setting specific to THE OCCUPATION is occupational.
- When in doubt, answer true. Reserve false for activities a reader would recognise as another occupation's job.

Reply with ONE JSON object and nothing else:
{"occupational": true or false,
 "primarily_performed_by": [titles of the related occupations, exactly as listed above, whose work the activity primarily is; [] when "occupational" is true],
 "rationale": "one sentence"}
